The library opens at nine, but the students arrive at eight.
They wait on the steps with their coffee and their notes, and they talk about the exam.
The exam is on Friday. The exam is always on Friday.
Some of them say the exam will be easy, and some of them say it will be hard.
Nobody knows, and the coffee gets cold.
At nine the doors open, and the students go in, and the steps are empty again.
In the evening they come out, tired, and the steps fill up with talk once more.
Was it a good day? Some say yes. Some say no. The exam is still on Friday.
